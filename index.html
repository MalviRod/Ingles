Community Questions & Practical Advice

Posted by Tariq H. Disability Support

"What is the best way to ask a colleague with speech impairment if they need me to repeat something?"

I work with a brilliant designer who has cerebral palsy and speech hesitation. Sometimes I don't catch a sentence clearly. Is it better to ask them to repeat, write it down, or guess? I don't want to make them feel uncomfortable.

💚 Verified Advice by Maya (Disability Ally):

"Directness with warmth is best! Say calmly: 'I missed the last few words, could you repeat that for me?' Never pretend to understand or fill in their sentences. They will appreciate your genuine effort to listen carefully!"

Posted by Sofia R. Cultural Sensitivity

"How can I handle family members who make casual discriminatory comments about other cultures?"

During holiday dinners, some older relatives make generalized comments based on stereotypes. How can I intervene peacefully without creating a huge argument, but standing firm against discrimination?

💚 Verified Advice by Dr. Kenji O.:

"Try the curiosity method: Ask 'What makes you say that?' or 'I've met many people from that background who are wonderful individuals, let's not generalize.' Reframing with personal human connection breaks stereotypes gently."
