Ask the community

No question is too small — this is a judgement-free space.

Is it okay to ask someone about their traditional dress if I'm genuinely curious?

Amara, Lagos: Yes — most people love sharing this. A good rule is to ask about the meaning, not just touch the fabric or take a photo without asking first.

I was invited to a friend's Lunar New Year dinner. What should I bring?

Wei, Singapore: Fruit in even numbers is a safe, appreciated gift. Avoid clocks or anything sharp — they carry unlucky meanings in some households.

How do I pronounce names correctly without being awkward about it?

Fatima, Karachi: Just ask once, kindly: "I want to say this right — could you say it for me?" Most people are glad you asked instead of guessing.
